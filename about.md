## Our mission

To promote digital and financial literacy in our school community through a
collaborative, student-run website.

## What we do

- Teach beginner coding through short, practical tutorials.
- Explain basic investing and economic ideas in simple language.
- Build interactive tools anyone can try.
- Give students real experience in writing, research, teamwork and web development.
